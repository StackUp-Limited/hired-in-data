Scholarship terms and eligibility

These are the terms for the Hired in Data Scholarship. They are written to be read, not to be survived.

By submitting an application you are agreeing to what follows.

What is being awarded

One place in the Hired in Data coaching programme, together with additional 1:1 coaching sessions. The winner pays nothing — no fee, no deposit and no card details are taken at any point.

The place is personal to the winner and cannot be transferred, deferred to a later cohort, or exchanged for cash or any other alternative.

Who can enter

You must be 18 or over, and you must have genuine experience or qualifications in data, analytics or machine learning. The programme helps people who can already do the work get hired for it; it does not teach the fundamentals.

You must be living in North America, the United Kingdom, Western Europe, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore or the United Arab Emirates.

Employees of Hired in Data, Code to Careers and Smarter Techies, and their immediate families, cannot enter. Current clients cannot enter.

Applications from outside these criteria will not be scored.

How to enter, and by when

One application per person, submitted through the form at hiredindata.com/scholarship. Applications close at 23:59 UK time on 28 August 2026. Late or duplicate applications are not considered.

There is no cost to enter and no purchase of any kind is necessary.

How the winner is chosen

This is a competition judged on merit. It is not a lottery and there is no element of chance.

Mandy Liu and Egor Howell score every eligible application against four published criteria: the applicant's readiness for the role they are targeting, the scale of the change landing it would make, their likely commitment to the programme, and their willingness to have their progress documented. Shortlisted applicants are invited to a short call before the final decision.

The judges' decision is final and no correspondence will be entered into about it. A runner-up is selected privately and will be offered the place if the winner declines it, cannot be contacted within seven days, or does not sign the participation agreement.

When you'll hear

Shortlisted applicants will be contacted during the week beginning 1 September 2026. The winner will be announced publicly on 2 September 2026.

Every eligible applicant will receive an email letting them know the outcome.

What the winner agrees to

Before the announcement, the winner signs a short participation agreement covering three things: attending the coaching sessions, doing the work between them, and allowing their progress to be documented and published.

Documentation means their first name and surname, filmed check-ins through the programme, and their eventual result including role and salary movement. This is a condition of the award, not an optional extra — it is the reason the place is free.

If the winner stops attending without agreement, the place may be withdrawn and offered to the runner-up.

Your information

The information you give us is used to judge your application, and to send you emails from Hired in Data. By applying you are agreeing to receive those emails. You can unsubscribe from any of them, at any time, using the link in the footer.

We do not sell your data or pass it to third parties for their own marketing. Your application is stored in our email and CRM systems and is retained for two years, after which it is deleted.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by replying to any email we send you.

We can end or change the scholarship

We reserve the right to suspend, cancel, amend or withdraw the scholarship at any time, and to change these terms, including the closing date, the number of places and the judging criteria.

We also reserve the right to reject any application, to withdraw an award before or after it is announced if the winner is found to be ineligible or to have given misleading information, and to decline to award the scholarship at all if no suitable applicant is found.

If we cancel or materially change the scholarship we will say so publicly and email everyone who has applied. No entrant has any claim against us in respect of any of the above.

Other things worth saying

The scholarship does not carry the money-back guarantee offered on the paid programme, for the obvious reason that no money changes hands.

This scholarship is run by Code to Careers. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
