Before you spend ten minutes on this

Is this for you?

We'd rather tell you now than waste your time. The programme helps people who can already do the work get hired for it — it does not teach data from scratch, so the entry bar is real.

You're eligible if

  • You already work in data, analytics or machine learning, or you have real experience in it
  • You have the fundamentals — Python, SQL, and the statistics behind them
  • You're based in North America, the UK, Western Europe, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore or the UAE
  • You're an individual contributor or a manager, not a Director or above
  • You can commit around 10 hours a week
  • You're willing to have your progress documented publicly

You're not eligible if

  • You're still learning the basics, or switching into data from an unrelated field with no experience
  • You have no work experience and also need visa sponsorship
  • You're a recruiter, or you sell to data teams
  • You coach, teach or build an audience around breaking into data
  • You want the result without changing how you work

Applying when you're clearly outside this doesn't get you a sympathy vote. It just means your application will be unsuccessful, and neither of us gets anything out of it.

Questions before you apply

Is this really free?

Yes. The whole programme plus additional 1:1 coaching — over $6,000 of value — and the winner pays nothing. No fee, no deposit, no card details.

The only thing we ask for is the work and the permission to document it.

What's the catch?

Two, and we'd rather say them plainly.

First, you have to do the work: roughly 10 hours a week for the length of the programme. Second, we'll be documenting your progress publicly — filmed check-ins, your name, your result. That's the trade. We cover the cost, you let us show what happened.

How many places are you giving away?

One.

We considered more, and decided against it. Places in the programme are limited because the coaching is small-group, so every scholarship seat is a seat a paying client can't have.

Why are you doing this?

Mandy and Egor have been coaching separately for years, and have now joined forces under one brand. This is how we're marking it.

And we want to document the process — one person's search, start to finish, in public.

How do you choose the winner?

It's judged, not drawn. Mandy and Egor read every eligible application and score it on four things: whether you're ready for the role you're going for, how much there is to change, whether you'll do the work, and whether you'll talk honestly about it while we document it.

A shortlist gets a 20-minute call before the final decision.

When do I find out?

Applications close at 23:59 on 28 August. Shortlisted applicants hear from us during the week of 1 September, and the winner is announced on 2 September.

What happens if I don't win?

You'll hear from us either way — nobody's application just disappears into silence.

There's only one place, so most people won't get it. But we read everything, and if your application shows us something we can help with, we'll tell you what we'd do about it.

Can I just join the programme instead of applying?

Yes, and if you're ready now that's the faster route — you'd start immediately rather than waiting until September.

Book a free strategy call and we'll look at your resume, your target roles and where your search is breaking down. If the programme is a fit we'll tell you. If it isn't, you'll still leave knowing what needs to change.

I'm outside the eligible countries. Can I still apply?

We'd rather you didn't. Everything we teach comes from hiring and being hired in a specific set of markets — we know what recruiters there screen for, what the interview loops look like, and what the salary bands actually are. Outside those markets we simply don't have that experience, and coaching you on guesswork would waste your time.

That isn't a judgement about you or about those markets. It's about where our experience is, and where this particular programme works.
